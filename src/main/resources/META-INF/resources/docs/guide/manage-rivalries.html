Manage Rivalries ​

Who this is for: BOOKER or ADMIN — creating rivalries and steering feuds. Rivalry heat itself builds automatically from match results, no role needed.

A booker's guide to rivalries — the feuds that give your shows a reason to exist. Rivalries build heat as wrestlers cross paths, force themselves onto your cards, and resolve in big matches.

How heat works (the ATW RPG rules, applied automatically):

  • 10 heat — they must wrestle at the next show. The game flags the match as must-book.
  • 20 heat — the rivalry can start ending: each show rolls to resolve it.
  • 30 heat — the feud is forced into a stipulation match (steel cage, hardcore, …) — the blowoff.

Step 1 — Create a rivalry ​

Open Rivalries from the navigation and click New Rivalry. Pick the two wrestlers and save — the rivalry starts at 0 heat and active.

You can't create a duplicate of a pair that's already active — one feud at a time per pairing.

Step 2 — Let heat build (or push it yourself) ​

Heat rises on its own as the two wrestlers:

  • Face each other in matches — winners and losers both gain, scaled by match importance.
  • Cut promos targeting each other — verbal jabs add heat without a physical contest.
  • Interfere, get interfered on, or hit drama events — anything that throws them together.

Every heat change is recorded as a heat event with a reason — open the rivalry's detail page to see the full history and understand why a feud escalated.

Step 3 — Book the matches ​

The show-planning tools work the heat for you:

  • The Add Segment dialog suggests your hottest unbooked rivalries ("Suggested feuds (by heat)") so the must-book matches don't slip.
  • Card validation warns when two wrestlers with a hot rivalry are booked without a stipulation.
  • The Show Planning view flags shows that would leave a heat-10 rivalry unbooked.

Step 4 — Let it resolve (or cool down) ​

Two things end a rivalry:

  • Resolution matches — at 20+ heat, PLE matches between the two roll for resolution; a win ends the feud. On premium shows the threshold drops (rivalries are meant to blow off on the big stage), and a rivalry that keeps missing resolution attempts on PLEs is force-resolved to keep feuds from stalling.
  • Heat decay — rivalries that stop being booked cool off gradually. If nothing re-ignites them, they fade to a natural end.

Both are game-settings driven — admins tune the decay interval, decay amount, minimum resolution heat, and whether regular shows (vs PLEs only) can resolve feuds under Admin → Game Settings.

Step 5 — Turn a rivalry into a story arc ​

From the rivalry's detail page, click Story Arc to launch the feud wizard — the structured, scripted way to run the feud (see Build Story Arcs). Rivalries handle the heat; story arcs handle the script.

Where to go next ​

  • Book Your First Show — putting the feud on a card.
  • Build Story Arcs — scripting the feud beat by beat.
  • The auto-generated Booker Overview documents every rivalry screen.
