# Report Match Results

A player's guide to reporting the results of matches booked for your wrestler — the heartbeat of league play. When a league match featuring your wrestler is booked, you get an inbox notification asking you to report who won.

**Who this is for:** players with a [player-controlled wrestler](./set-player-controlled-wrestler) whose match is up. Booker-run shows don't need this — the show adjudication decides those.

---

## Step 1 — Find the match report request

Open your **Inbox** from the navigation. Booked matches land as notification items with a **Report Result** button. Each item names the match and your opponent.

![Match reporting](/screenshots/league-match-report.png)

---

## Step 2 — Report the winner

Click **Report Result**. The report dialog shows the match's participants:

1. Pick the **Winner** from the participants list.
2. Click **Submit**.

That's it — the result flows into league standings immediately: wins, losses, and draws update on the league dashboard, and your opponent sees the outcome too.

> 💡 **Report promptly.** League play stalls while matches await results — the standings can't settle until every booked match has a winner. If you can't play, coordinate with your commissioner.

---

## Step 3 — Watch the standings

On the **League** page, the dashboard shows current standings (wins, losses, draws per wrestler) and complete rosters for all players. Your reported results update these live.

![League standings](/screenshots/league-standings.png)

---

## Troubleshooting

- **No Report Result button on an inbox item** — the item isn't a match-fulfillment notification, or the match was already reported. Only matches awaiting a result show the button.
- **Dialog won't submit** — a winner must be selected; the dialog won't accept an empty pick.
- **Wrong wrestler asked to report** — the request goes to the account controlling each participant. See [Set a Player-Controlled Wrestler](./set-player-controlled-wrestler) if the wrong account holds the link.
