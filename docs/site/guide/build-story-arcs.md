# Build Story Arcs

A booker's guide to story arcs — scripted, multi-show feuds with a planned beginning, middle, and blowoff. Where [rivalries](./manage-rivalries) track _heat_, story arcs are the _script_: beats you plan in advance and book onto shows in order.

---

## Step 1 — Launch the arc wizard

Story arcs hang off a rivalry. From an active rivalry's detail page, click **Story Arc** — the wizard opens pre-seeded with the two wrestlers.

![Story arc wizard](/screenshots/booker-story-arc-wizard.png)

### Wizard settings

- **Wrestlers** — two for a classic rivalry, **3+ for a multi-wrestler feud** (a stable implosion, a three-way dance for affection).
- **Arc Name** — what the card reads on show pages.
- **Length** — **Short** (1 PLE), **Medium** (2), or **Long** (3). Length sets how many premium shows the arc spans; beats pace between them.

---

## Step 2 — Plan the beats

A **beat** is one chapter of the story — a promo confrontation, an attack, a tag match that goes wrong, a betrayal. The arc's beats become your booking backlog: each one lands on a show before the next PLE in the arc's timeline.

On the arc's detail page:

- **Add Beat** — describe what happens and who's involved; each beat lists its participants and their role in the story.
- **Edit beats** — reorder and rewrite as the story evolves; nothing is locked until booked.
- **Beat status** — beats move from planned to booked as you attach them to show segments.

![Story arc beats](/screenshots/booker-story-arc-beats.png)

![Add a beat](/screenshots/booker-story-arc-add-beat.png)

---

## Step 3 — Book the beats onto shows

Each booked beat becomes a segment on the appropriate show — a promo beat becomes a promo segment, a confrontation becomes a match. The show planning views surface which beats still need a home so the story never stalls between PLEs.

If the AI narration is configured, booked segments inside an active arc get narration that's aware of the script — commentary references the story so far instead of treating each match as an isolated event.

---

## Step 4 — Land the payoff

The arc ends at its final PLE, where the story resolves:

- **Winner control** — when creating the arc you choose who decides the ending: **the booker picks** the winner (you're telling a specific story) or **the AI picks** (the rolls and momentum decide, and you book to whatever happened).
- After the payoff, the arc flips to **completed** and its full beat history stays readable from the arc list.

Saved arcs render as cards on the Story Arcs page — active arcs first, completed history below.

![Story arc card](/screenshots/booker-story-arc-card.png)

---

## Rivalry or story arc?

|           | Rivalry (heat)                      | Story Arc (scripted)                      |
| --------- | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Driven by | Match outcomes and promos           | Your beat plan                            |
| Ends by   | Resolution rolls, heat decay        | The final PLE payoff                      |
| Best for  | Organic feuds that write themselves | Stories that need to hit specific moments |

They compose: a hot rivalry is the natural seed for an arc, and booking arc beats keeps the underlying rivalry's heat climbing.
