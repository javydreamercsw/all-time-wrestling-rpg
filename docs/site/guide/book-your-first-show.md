# Book Your First Show

A first-time booker's walkthrough: from an empty calendar to a completed show with results. Written for anyone holding the BOOKER role (admins can do all of this too).

**Before you start:** your admin must have set up the promotion — wrestlers on the roster, at least one arena, and (for AI narration) a working AI provider. See [Admin Getting Started](./admin-getting-started) if anything is missing.

---

## Step 1 — Create the show

Open **Show List** from the navigation and create a new show. You'll pick:

- **Show type** — the kind of event (weekly TV, premium live event, house show). The type shapes expectations: weekly shows build momentum, premium events are where titles change.
- **Date** — when the show runs on the calendar.
- **Arena** — where. Capacity feeds attendance and fan calculations.

![Show planning](/screenshots/booker-show-planning.png)

---

## Step 2 — Fill the card

A show is an ordered list of **segments**. Add segments one by one; each has a type, participants, and (for matches) a match type:

- **Matches** are the heart of the card: pick two or more wrestlers and a match type (singles, tag, triple threat… — the list comes from your enabled expansions). The winner is decided when the show runs.
- **Promos** give wrestlers mic time to build heat without a physical contest — use them between matches to pace the show.
- **Championship matches**: attach a title to a match to put it on the line. Champions who retain gain fans; losers lose them.

Watch the **proposed card** helpers as you build — the game flags problems before showtime (a wrestler booked twice, a stipulation that doesn't match the participants, a card with no main event).

![Proposed card](/screenshots/booker-show-planning-proposed-card.png)

---

## Step 3 — Designate the main event

The last non-promo segment on the card is the night's headline. Main-event winners earn **elevated fan gains** and the AI narration treats the segment as the climax — so put your hottest rivalry there.

---

## Step 4 — Run the show

When the card is final, run the show. The game adjudicates each segment in order:

- Match outcomes roll from wrestler stats, health, stamina, and momentum — the better-prepared wrestler usually wins, but upsets happen.
- With an AI provider configured, every segment gets live narration and commentary you can share with your community (shows expose a QR code link for viewers).
- Fans, rankings, and injuries update as segments complete.

![Show QR share](/screenshots/booker-show-detail-qr-code-share.png)

---

## Step 5 — After the bell

Once the show is final:

- **Results are visible** to players and viewers — summaries update on the show card.
- **Fan reactions** land on winners and losers; a decisive win over a popular opponent moves the needle most.
- **Rivalries heat up or cool down** based on who fought whom and how.
- **Notifications** tell wrestlers and title holders what changed.

Check the show's detail page afterwards — it's also where you review the AI narration and segment results in order.
