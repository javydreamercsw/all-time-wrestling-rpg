# Grow a Wrestler

> **Who this is for:** BOOKER or ADMIN for the booking side (matches, title runs, contracts); PLAYER for the backstage actions of a player-controlled wrestler. Wear & tear, injuries, and fan gains apply to every wrestler automatically.

A booker's guide to the long game: fans, tiers, momentum, wear & tear, injuries, and contracts. Everything that makes a wrestler's career a story instead of a number.

**The growth loop:** wrestlers perform on shows → they gain (or lose) **fans** → fans drive their **tier** and their **salary** → matches cost **condition** → injuries and age bite → book wisely to keep stars healthy and rising.

---

## Fans and tiers

Fans are the currency of fame. Wrestlers gain them by:

- **Winning matches** — more for main-event wins, most for decisive wins over popular opponents.
- **Retaining or winning championships** — titleholders gain fans on every successful defense.
- **Winning tournaments** and headline moments.

Fans per wrestler are tracked **per universe**, so the same wrestler can be a legend in one universe and a rookie in another. Every show start snapshots each wrestler's fan count for the season's historical record.

Tiers rank the roster from **Rookie** up through **Icon**. Tier gates championship prestige — a title's tier only considers wrestlers at or above it — and drives the rankings ladder contenders climb toward title shots.

## Health, momentum, and status cards

Each wrestler carries live combat stats shown (with all modifiers applied) on their profile and the Player Dashboard:

- **Health** — the pool that matches and bumps deplete. A hurt wrestler performs worse and risks injury.
- **Stamina** — spent by match length and stipulations; recovered between shows.
- **Momentum** — the hot-streak meter. Winners ride it, losers lose it — momentum swings make underdog runs possible.
- **Effective stats** — hover any stat to see the full breakdown: campaign bonuses, injuries, bumps, and active Status Cards. Green badges are positive, red are negative.

## Wear & tear

Matches leave a mark. **Physical condition** degrades every match based on:

- **Match intensity** — Extreme rules double the wear.
- **Positioning** — main events demand more than openers.

Low condition means health penalties in the ring and, at the extreme, **forced retirement**. Condition recovers slowly on its own; booking a worn-down star every week is how you lose them. Admins can toggle the whole wear-and-tear system under **Admin → Game Settings**.

---

## Injuries

Big hits and brutal stipulations can injure a wrestler:

- Injuries carry a **severity** with a health penalty and a **recovery time** — the wrestler is sidelined or weakened until healed.
- Severity types are admin-configurable under **Admin → Injury Management** (name, severity, recovery days).
- Players speed recovery with **Recovery** backstage actions; bookers just have to wait — or avoid the hardcore match with their top star the week after a cage.

![Injury management](/screenshots/admin-injury-types.png)

## Backstage actions

Player-controlled wrestlers can take one **backstage action** per day:

- **Training** — push stats upward.
- **Recovery** — heal faster from injuries and condition damage.
- **Promo** — build hype (and heat with rivals) without getting physical.

Choices matter — the action spends the day, so a wrestler training can't simultaneously recover.

---

## Contracts and the cost of fame

Salaries are **dynamically tied to fan count** — a rising star costs more every month they rise. The GM Dashboard surfaces budget, roster health, and locker-room morale so you can see the bill coming.

The strategic tension: you must invest in developing new talent to afford keeping the superstars you built. Let a veteran's fans stagnate and their contract stays cheap; push a prospect to the main event and budget for their raise.
