# Manage Universes

> **Who this is for:** ADMIN only — creating universes, generating invites, and approving join requests are admin-gated. Members of a universe just switch between the ones they belong to via the sidebar selector.

An admin's guide to universes — isolated continuity containers for your promotions. Every show, wrestler state, rivalry, and title reign belongs to one universe, so you can run parallel timelines ("what if the Attitude Era never ended?"), separate campaigns per friend group, or a global roster plus private test beds without data bleeding between them.

---

## Step 1 — Create a universe

Open **Universes** from the navigation and click **Create Universe**. Give it a name and pick its **type** — the type shapes what the universe is for (a global shared world vs a private league space).

Everything you create while a universe is active — shows, seasons, tournaments, rivalries, wrestler stats — lives inside it.

---

## Step 2 — Switch between universes

The **Active Universe** selector in the sidebar is the master switch. Everything you see — show lists, rankings, tournaments, dashboards — filters to the selected universe. Switching is instant and safe: nothing is shared or copied, each universe is its own continuity.

Wrestler fans are tracked **per universe**, so the same wrestler can be a rising rookie in one universe and a reigning Icon in another.

---

## Step 3 — Invite members

Universes have membership. From the Universes page, admins generate **invite links**:

1. Open the invite management dialog and generate a link for the member type you want (player, booker, …).
2. Share the link — it opens a **Join Universe** page where the visitor creates (or links) an account and submits a join request atomically.
3. Approve join requests from the same dialog.

![Invite management](/screenshots/league-creation.png)

Members only see the universes they belong to — the universe selector lists accessible universes, not all of them.

---

## Step 4 — Scope the content

Each universe carries its own content switches:

- **Expansion settings** — which content sets are active in this universe, independent of other universes (see [Admin Getting Started](./admin-getting-started) for expansions).
- **Wrestler exclusions** — bar specific wrestlers from a universe's draft pools and eligibility.

---

## Step 5 — Back it up or take it with you

The **Export/Restore Images** tools on the Universes page handle universe artwork for backups and moves. For full data, **Export Universe Data** produces a portable dump of the universe's content — useful for archiving a completed season's continuity or sharing a setup with another install.

---

## When to use more than one universe

- **Test changes safely** — try a new ruleset in a sandbox universe before touching your main continuity.
- **Separate friend groups** — each league table gets its own universe so standings and histories never mix.
- **Alternate timelines** — branch a promotion at a dramatic moment and run both futures.

> 💡 **Keep the number sane.** Every universe multiplies the states the game tracks per wrestler. Most tables run two or three: main, sandbox, and (if leagues are in play) one per league.
