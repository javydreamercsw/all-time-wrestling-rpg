# Set a Player-Controlled Wrestler

An admin or booker's guide to handing a wrestler to a player. A wrestler flagged as **player-controlled** belongs to a real account: that player makes their wrestler's campaign choices, books their league matches, and gets personal notifications — instead of the AI running them as an NPC.

---

## Step 1 — Open the wrestler

Open **Wrestler List** from the navigation and click the wrestler you want to hand over (or create a new one). The edit dialog opens.

![Wrestler list](/screenshots/admin-wrestler-list.png)

---

## Step 2 — Flag the wrestler as a player

In the edit dialog:

1. Tick **Is Player**.
2. A **Player Account** combo appears — pick the account that will control this wrestler. Only players with an account can log in and act as their wrestler.
3. Save.

The roster list shows an **Is Player?** column so you can see at a glance who's human.

---

## Step 3 — What changes for a player-controlled wrestler

- **Player Dashboard** — the player sees their wrestler's stats, effective stats (with all modifiers applied), status cards, season history, and notifications there.
- **Campaign mode** — the player can assign the wrestler to a campaign and make story choices on their behalf; AI-controlled wrestlers never appear in a player's campaign slot.
- **Leagues** — player-controlled wrestlers can be drafted into leagues and their players report match results (see [Report Match Results](./report-match-results)).
- **Tournaments** — player-controlled entrants can appear in brackets; when one of their matches books, they get a notification to report the result.

---

## Step 4 — Take the wrestler back (un-flagging)

Untick **Is Player** and save. The link to the player account is cleared — the wrestler returns to AI control. Any campaign or league history stays with the wrestler.

> 💡 **One account, one wrestler:** the player-account link is exclusive — if an account already controls a wrestler, reassigning it moves control. Clear a wrestler's flag before handing the account to someone else.
